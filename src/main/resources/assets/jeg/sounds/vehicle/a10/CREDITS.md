# A-10 sounds

Built from recordings on Freesound (https://freesound.org). Each is adapted: mono, 44.1 kHz, filtered, levelled, and
looped or cut as described. The original recordings remain the work of their authors.

- `engine.ogg` (engine loop) and `engine_start.ogg`: **A-10 Thunderbolt Maximum RPM.wav**, by **SoundFX.studio**,
  Freesound sound **456267**, https://freesound.org/people/SoundFX.studio/sounds/456267/ . A recording of the A-10's
  TF34-GE-100 turbofans. Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0**,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ : non-commercial use only, like the SBW assets this project already
  uses. Made seamless with a crossfade; the start-up plays it back with its speed swept up from a crawl to idle, with a
  low rumble at light-off.
- `flyby1-3.ogg`: **A-10.ogg**, by **qubodup**, Freesound sound **205582**, https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/205582/ .
  A-10 flybys from a US Government video, dedicated to the public domain (**Creative Commons Zero 1.0**). Three clean
  passes (no gunfire): 0-4 s, 21-26.5 s and 40.5-46.5 s.
- `flyby4.ogg`: **A-10 Thunderbolt "Warthog" Jet Flyby.flac**, by **qubodup**, Freesound sound **189644**,
  https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/189644/ . Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
- `cockpit_flight.ogg`: **Plane Landing.mp3**, by **courter**, Freesound sound **447826**,
  https://freesound.org/people/courter/sounds/447826/ (**Creative Commons Zero 1.0**). The steady in-flight cabin hum,
  6-17 s, low-passed and looped.
- `runway_roll.ogg`: **Car rolling on asphalt .wav**, by **orlandorizo**, Freesound sound **591079**,
  https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/591079/ (**Creative Commons Zero 1.0**). 30-40 s, darkened and looped.
- `gear.ogg`: **Landing Gear**, by **77Pacer**, Freesound sound **425273**,
  https://freesound.org/people/77Pacer/sounds/425273/ (**Creative Commons Zero 1.0**).
- `touchdown1-3.ogg`: tyre squeals from **Chrysler LHS tire squeal 01**, by **audible-edge**, Freesound sound **71736**,
  https://freesound.org/people/audible-edge/sounds/71736/ (**Creative Commons Zero 1.0**), and **Tires.wav**, by
  **oldestmillennial**, Freesound sound **538213**, https://freesound.org/people/oldestmillennial/sounds/538213/
  (**Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ ). Short squeals pitched down for
  the bigger wheels, two to a landing, over the gear's lock clunk (425273) and a scrub of tyres on tarmac (591079).
- `fire.ogg` (the gun): from Superb Warfare (SBW); the original SBW attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms apply as
  documented in the project README. `gun_stop.ogg` is its last half second with an echo made from it.
- `gun_reload_start.ogg` and `gun_reload_end.ogg`: the GAU-8 reloading, mixed from this mod's own reload sounds
  (rocket launcher lid, magazine, bolt), pitched down for a big gun.
- `stall_warning.ogg` and `pull_up.ogg`: synthesised for this mod (the cockpit's stall warble and pull-up alarm are
  electronic tones).
- `*_cockpit.ogg` (`fire`, `gun_stop`, `engine`, `engine_start`, `runway_roll`, `gear`, `touchdown1-3`): the pilot's
  versions of the sounds above, made from them (same sources and licences): low-passed and given more low end, as heard
  through the airframe and canopy. The cockpit gun also carries a 65 Hz buzz at the GAU-8's rate of fire, synthesised.
