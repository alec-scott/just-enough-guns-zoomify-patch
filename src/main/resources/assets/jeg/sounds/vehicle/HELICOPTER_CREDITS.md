# Helicopter recordings

- AH-6 startup and flight: **Explorer Helicopter Engine Start Up Ignition Sequence and Idle .wav**, by **Paul368**, Freesound sound **410604**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/Paul368/sounds/410604/ . Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Adapted from the supplied recording: mono conversion, filtering, normalized gain, startup time compression (25–57 seconds shortened to 8 seconds), and a 46-second flight loop from 72–120 seconds with a two-second crossfade to avoid a short repeating/restarting engine texture.
- MI-28 startup and flight: **eurocopter b2.wav**, by **joly1**, Freesound sound **81454**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/joly1/sounds/81454/ . Dedicated under **Creative Commons Zero 1.0**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Adapted from the supplied recording: mono conversion, filtering, normalized gain, startup time compression (12–40 seconds shortened to 12 seconds), and a crossfaded flight loop from the user-selected 1:26–1:36 section (86–96 seconds).
- CH-47 startup, flight and blade slap, and the turbines spooling down on shutdown: **UH-1 "Huey" Iroquois Helicopter**, by **OroborosNZ**, Freesound sound **157722**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/OroborosNZ/sounds/157722/ . Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Adapted from the recording: mono conversion, slowed to 0.93 with the low end raised (a heavier helicopter), filtering, normalized gain, the startup cut from 0.5-15.5 s and 22-27.5 s with a delayed copy of the turbine whine for a second engine, a flight loop of two stretches (122-146 s and 148-172 s) laid over each other, a slap loop from the closest pass (242-253.5 s), and the spool-down played backwards from 5-24 s (design/chinook/prepare_audio.py).
- CH-47 distant rotor: **Helicopter Landing and Idle**, by **Fission9**, Freesound sound **693866**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/693866/ . Dedicated under **Creative Commons Zero 1.0**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Adapted: mono conversion, slowed to 0.93, filtering, levelled and soft-limited, a loop from 22-60 s.
- CH-47 ramp, crew door and gun window (design/chinook/prepare_audio.py doors()), all dedicated under **Creative Commons Zero 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), adapted by mono conversion, filtering, trimming, fades and normalized gain:
  - ramp hydraulics (loop): **piranha_rampe.mp3**, by **granconill**, Freesound **156360**, 0.5-10.5 s, https://freesound.org/people/granconill/sounds/156360/
  - ramp lock: **Motors_Hydraulic_Handycap_Ramp**, by **SoundsofTheMachine**, Freesound **784981**, 10.0-11.8 s, https://freesound.org/people/SoundsofTheMachine/sounds/784981/
  - crew door opening: **Train/Container - Sliding Door**, by **Bowesy**, Freesound **522127**, 0-2.8 s, https://freesound.org/people/Bowesy/sounds/522127/
  - crew door shutting: **Ford 115 T350 - Side door sliding shut**, by **Sadiquecat**, Freesound **819037**, 0.3-2.4 s, https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/819037/
  - boarding step landing: **Box with Metal Latches Hit 2.wav**, by **suspensiondigital**, Freesound **389705**, played at 0.8, https://freesound.org/people/suspensiondigital/sounds/389705/
  - ramp warning beep: synthesised for this project (a 2.4 kHz piezo warning tone, design/chinook/prepare_audio.py ramp_beep()), no recording
  - gun window: **window metal heavy slide open close creak**, by **kyles**, Freesound **407326**, 1.45-2.75 s, https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/407326/
- AH-6 gun: adapted from the existing SBW-derived A-10 firing asset, converted into a seamless mono firing loop at 80% amplitude. Original SBW attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms continue to apply as documented in the project README.

The original recordings remain the work of their respective authors. The adaptations are used for positional startup/engine audio; Doppler pitch changes are applied at runtime.
