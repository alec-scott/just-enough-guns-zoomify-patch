# Minigun vent hiss (`vent.ogg`)

Mixed from two recordings, both dedicated to the public domain under **Creative Commons Zero 1.0**,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ :

- **Steam.wav**, by **sniperous**, Freesound sound **365071**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/sniperous/sounds/365071/ . A steam iron letting off steam: the body and tail of the hiss (0.45 to 4.35 seconds of the recording).
- **Steam/hiss**, by **wubitog**, Freesound sound **234782**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/wubitog/sounds/234782/ . Air pressure being released: laid over the first 1.3 seconds as the opening burst.

Adapted: mono conversion, 44.1 kHz, 300 Hz high-pass, the burst faded out under the steam, 0.7 second fade at the end, normalized gain.
