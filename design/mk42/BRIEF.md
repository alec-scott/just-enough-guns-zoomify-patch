# Mk42 5"/54 gun mount brief

Status: **built and tested, not yet handed over, 2026-11-16.** The user said to continue the ports by the
guidelines; the decisions below were taken by default on the plan's recommendations and are listed at the end for
review. Model refit done ([refit.py](refit.py), [rig.json](rig.json)); what was played and tested is in
[IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md). Still to do: listening check, night pass, sandbox world.

- **Method:** [vehicle-development-guide.md](../../docs/vehicle-development-guide.md)
- **Standard to match:** the A-10 and helicopters; the mortar for the shell, its HUD and its report
- **Source:** Superb Warfare's `mk_42` (model, texture, numbers, sounds), used with the SBW team's permission; see
  [the port plan](../superbwarfare-port/PLAN.md).

## Purpose

- **What the player uses it for:** a fixed gun that defends a base, against the ground and the air. From day 80 the
  enemy brings BMP-2s, LAV-150s, AH-6s and Mi-28s; this answers all four at range. Direct fire with a ballistic arc
  out to about 3.7 km, so a gunner can also lob shells at a horde or a raid camp past the walls.
- **Success looks like:** a crew assembles it on the base wall, loads AP and HE from the hold, kills an enemy BMP-2
  at 400 blocks with AP, and brings down an AH-6 with HE bursting beside it.
- **Not Create Big Cannons.** CBC builds fixed guns from blocks that are loaded by hand and aimed by crank. This is
  one assembled mount with a gunner's seat, an automatic loader and aircraft-style fuzes.
- **Not the HPJ-11 CIWS** (fleet plan): that one is the close-in missile killer with automatic tracking. The Mk42 is
  crewed, slower firing and reaches much further.

## Real-world reference

- **Class:** US Navy 5"/54 caliber Mark 42 single gun mount (1950s destroyers and frigates).
- **Proportions:** gunhouse about 4.1 wide × 4.8 long × 3.6 high blocks with the sight hoods on top to 4.4 (refit:
  as SBW built it). Gun 6.43 blocks from trunnion to muzzle; the real bore is 6.86 m, of which part is inside the
  house. Scale 1.0.
- **Operator position:** inside the gunhouse; hidden, enclosed. The gun is laid from the sight hoods in local
  control: the camera rides the gun, just ahead of the gun port.
- **Moving parts:**

  | Part | Bone | Function |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Traverse | `turret` | the whole house turns; ±180° |
  | Elevation | `barrel` | -15° to +85° (real and SBW) |
  | Recoil | `gun_slide` | slides 11 px into the house on each shot and runs out (SBW's fire clip, driven from the synced shot time) |
  | Gun port shield | `gun_port_shield` | rides the cradle and closes the port as the gun elevates (part of `barrel`) |
  | Sight hoods, mount captain's hood | `sight_hood_left/right`, `mount_captain_hood` | cosmetic: where the pointer, trainer and mount captain look out |
  | Muzzle flash | `muzzle_flash` (LOD only) | drawn as particles in jegn |

- **Real performance:** 40 rounds a minute from two 20-round loader drums; muzzle velocity 808 m/s; 23 km range.
  Game: SBW's 15 blocks/tick and gravity 0.06 (about 3,750 blocks in a vacuum at 45°); 40 rounds a minute.
- **Start-up:** none.

## What to reuse

- **Rounds:** the shared shell (`ShellEntity`, built from the mortar round): falls under gravity exactly as the HUD
  predicts, keeps flying through unloaded terrain (FarFlight), bursts with blast falloff, refreshes Voxy terrain.
- **Fire control:** the mortar's predictor, shared (`ShellBallistics`, `ShellAim`): range, time of flight and an
  impact marker on loaded terrain.
- **Report:** the mortar's report, shared (`GunReportPayload`, `GunReportAudio`): aircraft distance curve to 1024
  blocks, near/distant crossfade, delayed by distance; the gunner hears the interior recording.
- **Seat, aim, ammo, reload, weapon switching:** the vehicle gun path every turret vehicle uses (`"turret"` data,
  hold ammo, magazine reload, `switchWeapon`).

## Vehicle data

- **Seats:** one, the gunner; `enclosed`, `hide_passenger`; pitch -85 to 15 (look up to 85°, down to 15°), yaw ±180.
- **Engine:** `static`.
- **Armed:** AP and HE (below). `max_health` 350 (SBW). Hold: `mini`.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Ammo | Behaviour | Damage (SBW's) | Blast / blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5"/54 AP (`vehicle_mk42_ap`) | `jeg:shell_5in_ap` | impact fuze | 450 direct | 120 within 3 blocks; blocks as power 3 |
| 5"/54 HE (`vehicle_mk42_he`) | `jeg:shell_5in_he` | impact fuze, plus a **proximity fuze** against aircraft, missiles and flying mobs (bursts within 4 blocks, armed after 10 ticks) | 250 direct | 120 within 10 blocks; blocks as power 6 |
| 5"/54 cluster (`vehicle_mk42_cm`) | `jeg:shell_5in_cm` | 3 ticks out, once a block or target is within 7 ticks of flight ahead, breaks into 18 bomblets thrown on at 0.4-0.6 of its speed, spread 8 (SBW) | bomblet 100 direct | bomblet 50 within 3 blocks; blocks as power 1.5 |
| 5"/54 grapeshot (`vehicle_mk42_gs`; incendiary since 2026-11-19, `Mk42Entity.INCENDIARY`) | `jeg:shell_5in_gs` | breaks up at the muzzle into 36 pellets at 0.9-1.1 of its speed, spread 5, flying 200 ticks (SBW) | 1,000/36 per pellet; every pellet counts | none |

- **Drum:** 20 rounds, 30 ticks between shots (40 a minute), 6 s to refill a drum from the hold.
- **Spread:** SBW's 0.05.
- **Projectile path:** `ShellEntity`, FarFlight, 400-tick life (a 45° shot lands in about 350).
- **Proximity fuze and friends:** like a real VT fuze it bursts near any aircraft, friendly ones included; the HUD
  says so. A player's own aircraft near the line of fire is at risk, as in reality.

## Decisions taken by default (2026-11-16), for review

1. **Damage:** SBW's numbers (AP 450 + 120 within 3; HE 250 + 120 within 10), as the mortar kept SBW's. Block
   damage HE power 6 (less than the mortar's 8, since it fires ten times faster).
2. **Proximity fuze on HE** (SBW has none; real Mk42 AA rounds had VT fuzes). It does not tell friend from foe.
3. **Rate of fire:** the real 40 a minute from a 20-round drum, rather than SBW's one round and a 1.5 s reload each
   shot.
4. **Ammunition:** AP, HE, cluster and grapeshot (user, 2026-11-16: include cluster and grapeshot).
5. **Obtaining:** vehicle assembling table; shells at a crafting table.
6. **Turret speed (user, 2026-11-16: add the slew limit):** the real mount's 40°/s train and 25°/s elevation
   (`"traverse_rate": 2.0, "elevation_rate": 1.25` in the turret data, shared code). The gunner looks freely; the
   server turns the gun toward the view, and a bore marker shows where the gun points until it has caught up.
