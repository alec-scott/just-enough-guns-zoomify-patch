# 105 mm howitzer implementation status (2026-11-16)

Brief: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

## Built

- **Model:** our own, generated by [generate.py](generate.py) (M101A1 class, 1:1): 25 bones, 155 cuboids, two LODs,
  paint mask, Blockbench project, review renders, the workbench icon and the two round icons. Validation checks the
  tyres on the ground, nothing dragging packed, the spades biting in deployed, the closed trails meeting, the breech
  clearing the ground and the trails over the whole elevation range at full recoil, and the cradle passing the
  shield's slot at full depression. Hitboxes (packed and deployed, trails in segments) are written into
  `data/jeg/vehicles/howitzer.json`.
- **Entity:** `HowitzerEntity` on `ShellGunEntity`: three charges (`ballistics()` follows the selected one), HE and
  smoke, deploy state (N), travel lock, trail shifting with A/D, variable recoil, the fire mission and its panel rows.
  `HowitzerRig` mirrors `rig.json`.
- **Shared, new:**
  - `VehicleEntity`: `canFireWeapons`, `turretTravelLock`, `towEye`, `canBeTowed`, `hasTowKeyAction`/`towKey` (the tow
    key now reaches any vehicle that uses it).
  - The FSV-6's hitch tows a vehicle with a tow eye trail first (the Type-63 too, `Type63Entity.TOW_EYE`).
  - `FireSolution` (low and high arcs by bisection on the real flight), `ShellGunEntity.panelRows`.
  - `ObserverMarker` and `ObserverMarkPayload` (spyglass + attack), `/justEnoughGuns firemission <x> <z>`.
  - Smoke rounds: `ShellEntity.Warhead.smokeShell`, `SmokeGrenadeEntity.smokeScreen`, and a world-lit
    `smoke_screen` particle kept going for the screen's life and sent to 512 blocks.
  - `RetiredVehicleEntity`: the waveforce tower turns into a chest of its hold; its assembly recipe is removed.
- **Data:** assembly recipe (32 iron, 2 iron blocks, 8 copper, 2 black dye, 4 redstone), round recipes (HE: iron,
  TNT, copper, makes 2; smoke: iron, smoke grenade, copper), language, Carry On blacklist, stand-in sounds.

## Played (mcpfabric, pack client with Photon)

- Looked at it packed from the front, side, rear and above; boarded with a real right-click; loaded HE and smoke
  through the crate screen; deployed with N; marked a target through a spyglass as an observer; set a fire mission by
  command; laid on the panel's numbers (bearing and low arc "ON"); fired HE and smoke at 206 m; packed up; looked at
  it and the smoke at night.
- Found and fixed:
  - The smoke round's screen couldn't be seen from the gun: the clouds' own particles show only within entity range
    and campfire smoke is a block across. It now lays big grey puffs for the screen's life, sent to 512 blocks.
  - Those puffs first used the flare's particle, drawn full bright: under shaders they came out orange and would
    glow at night. A new `smoke_screen` particle takes the world's light.
  - The shield's stencil was clipped ("105MI"); the observer's message said "1 guns".
- Also fixed on the way, not the howitzer's: the Mk42's 5-inch HE and AP recipes declared an unused key, so the game
  refused them and those shells couldn't be crafted.

## Finalisation (2026-11-18, design/READINESS.md)

- **Its own report from real recordings** (`prepare_audio.py`), replacing the Mk42's pitched down: a field artillery gun
  fired close by (InMotionAudio, "69th Field Artillery Gun", CC0) up close and for the crew; three rounds of a US Army
  piece in the field with their roll off the ground (qubodup, from Army footage, CC0) as the distant layer. Loading
  stays the Mk42's (SBW, breech and rammer).
- **Weight:** `"mass"` 2.1 t (the M101A1's); the FSV-6 pulls away slower with it on the hitch.
- Wrecks as every gun does (cooks off, burns; `GroundWreckClientGameTest`, the shared artillery path).

| Test (`-PwithPack`) | Result |

| `HowitzerClientGameTest` | passes, now also listening: `vehicle.howitzer.load` and `vehicle.howitzer.fire_interior` (the crew's take, the player at the gun) heard |

south, range, trails "Packed: N to deploy", charge, target) and the elevation scale read clearly in the dark; there are
no lamps on the real gun. The third-person camera sits low behind it, the gun at the bottom of the frame.
