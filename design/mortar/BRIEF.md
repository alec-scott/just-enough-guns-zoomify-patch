# M81 mortar brief

Status: **decided 2026-11-15** (answers at the end); being built. Model refit done ([refit.py](refit.py),
[rig.json](rig.json)).

- **Method:** [vehicle-development-guide.md](../../docs/vehicle-development-guide.md)
- **Standard to match:** the A-10 and helicopters
- **Worked example of the process:** the BD-40 bulldozer
- **Source:** Superb Warfare's mortar (model, texture, clips, ballistics), used with the SBW team's permission; see
  [the port plan](../superbwarfare-port/PLAN.md).

## Purpose

- **What the player uses it for:** dropping high-explosive rounds on targets the crew cannot see, 135–770 blocks
  away: a horde massing at night, an enemy BMP-2 or LAV-150 from day 80, a gunner raid's camp.
- **Success looks like:** a player takes the mortar out of their inventory, sets it up behind a wall in a few
  seconds, dials a range on the HUD, and walks rounds onto a target 400 blocks off that they found with a spyglass
  or the map. They then pack it up and move on.
- **Not Create Big Cannons.** CBC (on the server) builds fixed, multi-block guns from Create parts, including a
  drop mortar. This one is carried by one player and set up in seconds, for squads in the field.

## Real-world reference

- **Class:** 81 mm infantry mortar, M252 / L16 class.
- **Proportions:** tube 1.27 m (refit: 1.269 blocks); baseplate 0.51 × 0.51 blocks; bipod to 1.35 blocks high.
  SBW's model was 1.23× real size; the refit scales it by 0.815.
- **Operator position:** crouched behind the baseplate, open air. The gunner is visible.
- **Moving parts:**

  | Part | Bone | Function |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Tube elevation | `barrel` | 45–85°, set by the gunner; sets the range |
  | Traverse | entity yaw | the whole mortar turns; real traverse without re-laying the bipod is ±5°, here it turns freely but slowly |
  | Bipod | `bipod_pivot` | hinge angle from `rig.json` per elevation, so the feet stay on the ground (gap ≤ 0.015 blocks at 85°) |
  | Round | `shell` | shown dropping into the muzzle in the `fire` clip |
  | Fire-control screen | `fire_control` | the ballistic computer the HUD's range readout comes from; functional, always fitted |
  | Muzzle flash | `muzzle_flash` | LOD only in SBW; the flash is a particle in jegn |

- **Real performance:** 45–85° elevation; 5.6 km maximum range; 15 rounds a minute sustained, 30 for a short burst.
  Game: SBW's ballistics (10 blocks/tick, gravity 0.13) give about 770 blocks at 45° and 135 blocks at 85° in a
  vacuum. The test measures the real figure with jegn's projectile drag.
- **Start-up:** none. Deploying takes 2 s (set the baseplate, lay the bipod), packing up 2 s.

## What to reuse from the A-10 / helicopters

- **Rounds:** the long-range projectile path ("FarFlight") for the shell. It keeps flying through unloaded terrain,
  loads terrain ahead, sends tracers to 1024 blocks, and refreshes Voxy terrain after a blast (`DistantTerrainRefresh`).
- **Sound:** `AircraftSoundRange` for the firing report and the far-sound track (distant one-shot to 1024 blocks),
  delayed by distance, as the A-10 gun.
- **Camera and seat code:** `VehicleInputHandler.clampSeatView` and the HPJ-11's static engine (`"type": "static"`).
- **Not reused:** the turret-seat camera lock (`"turret": {"seat": 0}`): it would pin the view to a barrel
  pointing at the sky. The gunner looks freely and the tube follows the dialled elevation instead.

## Controls (same family as the HPJ-11, the other static weapon)

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Right-click (empty hand) | man the mortar (crouched gunner seat) |
| Mouse | look around freely |
| W / S | elevation up / down, 5°/s; the HUD shows range and time of flight |
| A / D | traverse left / right, 10°/s (the traversing handwheel) |
| R or left mouse button | load a round from the gunner's inventory: it drops and fires 1 s later |
| Right-click with a round (on foot) | a second player loads (the assistant gunner) |
| Sneak + right-click (empty hand, on foot) | pack up: returns the mortar item and any loaded round |

Changes from the HPJ-11, and why: no camera lock and no mouse-pitch aiming, because a mortar is aimed by bearing
and range, not by sight. Traverse is on A/D rather than following the view: jegn turns a rider's view with the
vehicle (`clampSeatView`), so a mortar following the view would chase its own gunner's camera.

## Vehicle data

- **Seats:** one, `driver: true`, open (`enclosed: false`, `hide_passenger: false`), crouched behind the
  baseplate; yaw ±180, pitch as an open seat.
- **Engine:** `static`, like the HPJ-11.
- **Armed:** one weapon, the 81 mm HE round (below). `max_health` 40 (SBW).

## Hitbox

From [rig.json](rig.json), generated by `refit.py`:

| OBB part | Model part it covers | Centre (x, y, z) | Size (w × h × d) | Armour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| body | baseplate | 0, 0.075, 0 | 0.51 × 0.15 × 0.51 | light steel |
| turret | tube and bipod over the whole 45–85° sweep | -0.009, 0.675, 0.657 | 0.68 × 1.35 × 1.51 | light steel; damage stops elevation changes |

- **Moving parts:** the tube box covers the sweep, so nothing visible is unhittable at any elevation. It includes
  some empty space at high elevation; acceptable for a 1.3-block weapon.
- **Entity `sized(w, h)`:** 0.8 × 1.4 (the baseplate plus the tube at 45°).

## Behaviour

- **Deploy:** right-click the ground with the mortar item. It sets up facing the player's view; 2 s with a
  deploy sound, and it can't be fired until set.
- **Fire drill:** load (R or right-click with a round): the `fire` clip drops the round, and 20 ticks later it fires
  from the muzzle point in `rig.json`, at the dialled elevation and the mortar's bearing. Spread 0.5 (SBW). At most
  one round in the tube; next load 60 ticks after firing, so a round every 4 s (15 a minute, the real sustained
  rate).
- **Limits:** no loading while setting up; no loading when a block stands within 3 blocks of the muzzle on the
  line of fire (`MortarEntity.tubeBlocked`; the round would burst on the crew's cover).
- **HUD:** bearing, elevation, predicted range and time of flight from the same step-by-step solver the round
  flies by (`MortarRig.predict`). An impact marker (particles at the predicted point, for the gunner only) shows
  when that point is in loaded terrain; beyond it the ground is assumed at the mortar's height, and the HUD says so.
- **Damage and wreck:** smoke under 30%; at 0 it breaks and drops nothing but scrap and the loaded round (SBW drops
  the deployer). No wreck entity: too small.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Projectile path | Range | Tracers / impacts far off | Recoil / heat / reload |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 81 mm HE round (`jeg:mortar_round`, SBW's shell icon) | `MortarRoundEntity`: FarFlight keeps it ticking and loads terrain ahead; gravity 0.13, 10 blocks/tick | 135–770 blocks | no tracer (real rounds carry none); the blast is sent to players out to 1024 blocks, with Voxy refresh | 40-tick drill |

**Blast (decided):** SBW's numbers: explosion radius 8 and 100 damage, falling off to nothing at 8 blocks (the
missiles' curve), plus 60 on a direct hit. Blocks break the way jegn's rockets break them (`WarheadBlockDamage` at
scale 1, a TNT-type explosion of power 8). Vanilla's own entity damage from that explosion is switched off, so the
damage is SBW's and not double counted.

## Lights

None; open seat. The HUD is readable at night. `openSeat`: yes.

## Interior, interaction and screens

Not applicable beyond the HUD. The round is loaded from the gunner's inventory (any slot), so no hidden input.

## Tests

- **`MortarClientGameTest`** (one focused class, real keys and clicks): deploys from the item with a right-click;
  2 s set-up; W/S elevation at 5°/s, stopping at 45° and 85°; A/D traverse at 10°/s; R loads, and the round leaves
  about a second later and uses one round from the inventory; a 45° shot flies about 760 blocks through unloaded
  terrain and lands where `MortarRig` and the HUD predict; an 85° shot lands on the HUD's marker on loaded terrain;
  the report is heard; SBW's blast (most of 100 at 2 blocks, eased off at 6, nothing outside 8 so no vanilla damage on
  top), stone broken and obsidian kept; a player on foot loads the round in their hand; sneak-click packs up and
  returns the item and the round in the tube.
- **Not yet covered:** the renderer's bipod pose captured in game (the refit checks it offline: feet within 0.015
  blocks); a far observer at 1100 blocks; Carry On in the pack.
- **Hitbox:** shots at the baseplate and tube hit, shots beside them miss (from more than 5 blocks).
- **Sound:** report heard at 1100 blocks through the far track, delayed by distance.
- **Pack runs:** `-PwithPack`, and the user's client closed first.
- **Sandbox world:** `Mortar range`: flat range with targets at 150, 300, 500 and 750 blocks, and a spyglass in
  the kit.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-15)

1. Aiming: **A**, range dial and HUD.
2. Blast: **SBW's radius 8 and 100 damage**; blocks break the way jegn's rockets break them.
3. Size: **real size** (0.815 of SBW's).
4. Obtaining: **crafted at a crafting table**, since it is more a weapon than a vehicle; deployed from the hand.
   Rounds are crafted at a crafting table too.

## Questions as asked

1. **How the gunner aims.**
   - **A. Range dial and HUD** (above): bearing by looking, range by W/S, impact marker. Recommended.
   - **B. A plus a forward observer:** another player marks a point with a spyglass and the mortar lays on it
     itself. Better shared with the Type-63 and PLZ-05, so it's proposed as a follow-up.
   - **C. SBW's way:** a rangefinder item writes "firing parameters" that you click onto the mortar, plus a
     fire-control screen upgrade that re-aims after each round.
2. **The blast.** SBW uses radius 8 and 100 damage (TNT is radius 4). Proposed: radius 5, 60 damage direct, and
   block damage following whatever jegn's rockets do on the server. Or should it never break blocks?
3. **Scale.** The refit brings the tube to real size (0.815 of SBW's). Keep, or use SBW's size?
4. **Obtaining it.** Crafted at the vehicle assembling table as an item, deployed from the hand (no container), and
   rounds crafted there too. OK?
