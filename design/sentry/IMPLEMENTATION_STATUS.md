# Perimeter sentry implementation status (2026-11-17)

Brief: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

## Built (pass 1)

- **Model:** generated by [generate.py](generate.py): 14 bones, 43 cuboids, a 128 × 32 texture, review renders, the
  workbench icon, the hitbox written into the data; the feet are checked against the ground.
- **Entity:** `SentryEntity` on the shared `AutoDefenceEntity` (with the HPJ-11): monsters as targets, those after a
  player or villager first; the line of fire is checked for anything friendly before each shot.
- **Render:** the head and gun; the belt empties in four steps as the hold runs down; the status lamp (green watching,
  amber tracking, red firing, red blinking out of ammunition).
- **Data:** the 7.62 mm gun (fed from the hold, the coax's report), a 9-slot hold, health 80, energy 200,000; the
  recipe; Carry On; language; the ArmourTest sandbox.
- **The laser tower is retired:** its type turns a saved tower into a chest of its hold (`RetiredVehicleEntity`, for one
  release); its recipe and workbench entry are gone.

## Finalisation (2026-11-18, design/READINESS.md)

- **Heard on automatic.** Unmanned fire (`VehicleEntity.fireUnmanned`, every automatic defence) spawned the round but
  sent no gun report, so a sentry holding a wall fired in silence. It now sends the report as manned fire does (the
  HPJ-11's rotary gun, which has no report set, is still heard through its loop).
- **Its own report:** a real IWI Negev NG-7 (a 7.62x51 machine gun) in full auto, recorded close outdoors
  (areniporgen, CC0): the burst's last round with its ring-out, as near, distant (low-passed, ground echo) and operator
  takes (`prepare_audio.py`, `vehicle.gun.sentry`). Before, it borrowed the coax's light-machine-gun sound. An M60
  recording was tried first and rejected: its rounds smear into one wash and no single round could be cut.
- **Out of ammunition:** on automatic with a target and an empty belt, the bolt clicks on an empty chamber every 0.6 s
  (`vehicle.sentry.dry_fire`, a real dry fire lowered for a heavier action), so a player hears which sentry to feed.
- **The status lamp is readable and glows:** one 1.6 px lamp on the camera (three 0.8 px dots could not be made out at
  3 blocks), drawn full bright by the glow layer (`sentry_glow.png`, from `generate.py`), green watching, amber
  tracking, red firing or blinking empty.
- **Log spam fixed:** GeckoLib logged "Unable to find animation: 'idle'" every frame for every sentry and HPJ-11 in view
  (10,858 lines in one test); both now play their rest pose.
- **Weight and feel:** the head's servos are heard slewing (`VehicleFeel`); `"mass"` 0.3 t (the FSV-6 feels it as tow
  load).
