# Leopard 2A7 implementation status (2026-11-17)

Brief: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

## Built

- **Model:** our own, generated by [generate.py](generate.py) at 1:1 (11.7 × 3.9 × 3.0 blocks with the gun forward):
  318 bones, 1,281 cuboids, two LODs, paint mask, Blockbench project, review renders, the workbench icon. Hull with
  glacis plates, side skirts (heavy at the front), seven road wheels a side with spokes and bolts, idler in front,
  toothed sprocket behind, four return rollers; a turret with stepped wedge armour on the cheeks (leaning back as it
  rises), bustle and baskets, the gunner's and commander's sights, smoke dischargers; the 120 mm with fume extractor
  and recoiling tube; the FLW 200 weapon station on its own yaw and pitch bones.
- **Tracks:** 60 links a side named for the shared driver, a belt under them, the path from the running gear in the
  data's `"tracks"`.
- **Validation (in the generator):** the links stand on the ground at any travel, nothing else touches it, the road
  wheels ride on the track, and the gun clears the hull (deck, glacis, skirts, fittings) at every elevation and
  traverse, except within 70° of straight back below level (the rear-deck rule).
- **Entity:** `Leopard2Entity` on `ShellGunEntity`: the Mk42's four shells at 40 blocks a tick, fired on the move; coax;
  the commander's 12.7 mm weapon station (`seatRidesTurret`, `seatGunMuzzle`, long-range rounds); the rear-deck
  depression limit.
- **Shared, new:** `VehicleEntity.clampTurretPitch(turretYaw, pitch)` (a slew-limited turret's elevation by bearing);
  `updatePintleGun` in the renderer (the PLZ-05's roof gun and the Leopard's weapon station); weapon-station muzzle
  flashes (`*_rws`).
- **Data:** assembly recipe (64 iron, 8 iron blocks, 24 copper, 20 redstone, 4 green dye), weapon stats, SBW's YX-100
  sounds, language, Carry On blacklist.

## Played (mcpfabric, pack client with Photon)

- Beside a BMP-2 by day from the front quarter and the side: opaque and lit, reads as a Leopard 2A5/A7 (wedge turret,
  skirts, long gun, weapon station).
- Boarded, loaded AP through the hold, switched to AP, fired while driving at 10 m/s: smoke at the muzzle, 3.5 s
  reload.
- Looked straight back and 25° down: the turret turned to the rear and the gun stayed level.
- Took the commander's seat, loaded the weapon station, fired. Found and fixed: it threw the cannon's fireball (its id
  matched no machine-gun rule); the guide now says to name weapon stations `*_rws`.
